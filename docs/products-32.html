Village Story Clay Horse Figure

₹1,850

A folk-inspired horse figure concept with an upright neck and patterned saddle. The maker builds the body in balanced sections, then adds surface marks before firing; display it indoors on a stable shelf. Potters prepare the clay, shape the walls, dry the form evenly and fire it. Handmade variations in shade, rim and profile are part of the process.

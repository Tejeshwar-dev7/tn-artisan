The reason behind the marketplace

Living craft deserves a living marketplace.

TN ARTISAN is an independent concept storefront designed to help Tamil Nadu makers introduce their work, process and place to people looking for handmade objects.

Explore the marketplace →
OUR MISSION

Make skilled work easier to find, understand and sustain.

Give artisans a practical digital storefront, a fair path to customer orders and space to share the story behind their work. Help customers discover by craft, material and district, and follow the order as it is made and delivered.

OUR VISION

A future where Tamil Nadu craft can thrive across generations.

We envision a trusted, accessible marketplace where makers retain authorship, customers understand what they are buying, and digital tools help preserve local knowledge and strengthen independent livelihoods.

FOR SMALL MAKERS

Less time piecing together a storefront.

Product pages, material details, price, estimated dimensions, customer orders and updates are brought together so makers can focus on the work and communicate clearly.

FOR NGOS AND COLLECTIVES

Practical support around the maker.

Community partners can help with photography, listing preparation, digital skills, packaging and fulfilment coordination, with the maker’s permission and story at the centre.

This is a student concept project. Product examples use the owner-supplied photographs and selected openly licensed craft reference photographs. Prices and dimensions are illustrative estimates, and live sales or NGO partnerships are not claimed.

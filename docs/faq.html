Help with shopping

Are product details final?

Some collection listings use estimated prices, dimensions and sample availability. The listing identifies concept details; confirm specifications, lead time and price with the maker before fulfilment.

How can I pay?

Choose cash on delivery or online checkout through Razorpay when the marketplace owner has configured the provider account. The online flow verifies the provider signature and captured payment before fulfilment. Test keys are for sandbox transactions only; this local preview has no merchant credentials and cannot take a real charge.

Can I use Google Pay?

Google Pay may appear as a UPI option in Razorpay Checkout when enabled for the merchant account. Direct production integrations require merchant onboarding and approval.

Where can I track an order?

Open and choose the order. Its timeline refreshes from recorded order events, and the map identifies the delivery locality. It does not show a courier's live GPS position; the maker adds dispatch, carrier and tracking details when available.

When can I review a product?

After delivery, sign in and open the marketplace product’s review section.

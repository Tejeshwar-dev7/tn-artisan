A floral temple-inspired relief panel concept with a central medallion and layered border. The carver removes material in measured stages, following the grain so delicate edges do not split. Carving begins with a planned outline and proceeds from broad cuts to fine detail, always working with the grain. Keep finished wood dry and away from heat.

Your craft, your story, your customer.

A maker-first workspace for independent Tamil Nadu artisans to present their work, receive orders and keep customers updated.

01

Build a maker profile

Share your shop name, craft, district and story. New profiles are reviewed by a marketplace administrator before listings go live.

02

List with useful detail

Add materials, technique, dimensions, price, stock and a product photo. Approved listings appear in the marketplace.

03

Manage each order

Accept paid or cash-on-delivery orders, record production milestones, and enter courier references and real shipment updates when available.

04

See your earnings

Paid online orders record the selling price, platform commission and artisan share in the maker earnings ledger.

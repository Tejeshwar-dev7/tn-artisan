Village Story Clay Horse Figure

CRAFT PASSPORT AC · TN · 32
▦

TN ARTISAN Studio

Curated concept collection · Tamil Nadu, Tamil Nadu

A folk-inspired horse figure concept with an upright neck and patterned saddle. The maker builds the body in balanced sections, then adds surface marks before firing; display it indoors on a stable shelf. Potters prepare the clay, shape the walls, dry the form evenly and fire it. Handmade variations in shade, rim and profile are part of the process. Prototype listing: price and dimensions are estimates for this project and must be confirmed with the maker before purchase.

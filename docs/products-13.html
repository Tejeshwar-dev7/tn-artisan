Kamadhenu Raised-Relief Art Panel

₹12,800

Inspired by devotional panels that pair a central figure with dense ornament, this piece is imagined as a Kamadhenu composition. Each relief ridge and painted accent is planned to catch light without crowding the central subject. Traditional Thanjavur panels are prepared in layers, with relief ornament and fine brushwork creating a luminous surface. Keep framed work away from damp and direct sunlight. Prototype listing: price and dimensions are estimates for this project and must be confirmed with the maker before purchase.
